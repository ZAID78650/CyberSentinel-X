# Security Policies

## Password Policy

- Minimum 12 characters, with letters and numbers.
- MFA is mandatory for all privileged accounts and recommended for all users.
- Passwords must not be reused across systems.
- Accounts should be locked after 10 consecutive failed attempts.
- Passwords must be changed immediately upon suspicion of compromise.

## Access Control Policy

- Least privilege: users receive only the access required for their role.
- Privileged access requires separate approval and is reviewed quarterly.
- Access to sensitive data (PII, financial, IP) is logged and monitored.
- Terminated employees' access is revoked within 24 hours.

## Acceptable Use

- Corporate credentials must never be used for personal services.
- Unknown USB media is prohibited on managed endpoints.
- Software installation requires approval from IT security.

## Incident Response Policy

- All suspected incidents are reported to the SOC within 1 hour.
- Evidence must be preserved; systems must not be powered off without
  approval.
- Incident reports are retained for at least 12 months.

## Data Classification

- Public: no restrictions.
- Internal: restricted to employees.
- Confidential: restricted to named roles, requires logging.
- Restricted: highest sensitivity (PII, trade secrets), requires approval for
  any access and full audit trail.
